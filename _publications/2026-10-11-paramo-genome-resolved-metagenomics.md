We recovered 109 high-quality metagenome-assembled genomes from three ecosites in Chingaza. Genomic analyses identified previously undescribed lineages and metabolic potential for acetogenic carbon fixation and sulfate reduction. Although assembled genomes lacked methanogenesis markers, read-level analysis detected a low-abundance methanogen signal concentrated in peatland soils.
