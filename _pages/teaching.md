## Research mentoring

I have mentored undergraduate researchers both at Penn State and at Cornell University. My mentoring has included hands-on training in molecular biology and cloning, troubleshooting experiments, reviewing analyses, and helping students organize their research projects. I have also coordinated assignments and timelines and provided feedback on their work.

In my current lab, I train researchers to use the metagenomic analysis pipeline I developed.
